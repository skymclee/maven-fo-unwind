Executive Summary
Day 1 Open delivers the strongest risk-adjusted return across all 607 deals — +4.80% mean, 93% positive, Sharpe 0.30 — the highest of any horizon. Returns decay monotonically from Day 2 onward in the aggregate, with the Sharpe falling from 0.30 at D1 Open to just 0.07 at D126. A statistically significant regional divergence (p=0.042) fundamentally changes the optimal holding period by market, driven by the Tokyo Stock Exchange's corporate governance reform creating sustained post-placement re-rating in Japan. The discount paradox — deeper discounts correlating with lower subsequent returns (r=−0.304) — reflects information asymmetry rather than snap-back opportunity.
  • HK/China: Exit at Day 1 Open (+6.35%, 91% positive). Mean turns negative by D63; median crashes to −9.61% at D126.
  • Japan: Hold to D63–D126. TSE governance reform drives persistent re-rating to +9.13% at D126 — the only market with a rising Sharpe beyond D1.
  • Marketed vs Overnight: D1 Close hit rate 95% vs 72%. By D126, marketed averages +8.58% vs overnight −2.45%.
  • Basic Materials: Strongest sector at D63 (+10.69%, n=26). Technology strong at D1 but mean-reverts negative by D63.
  • 2025 regime: 33% of sample with elevated returns (+8.61% D1 Open) — biasing multi-year averages upward.

Key Findings
  • Japan anomaly: Only market where Sharpe rises beyond D1 (0.49 → 0.32 at D126). TSE reform drives sustained re-rating. Divergence from HK/China significant at 5% level.
  • Basic Materials standout: +10.69% mean at D63 (n=26, 65% positive) — strongest sector beyond D5, driven by Japan commodities.
  • Discount paradox: r=−0.304 at D1. Deeper discounts signal placement risk — not snap-back. Do not size up on discount alone.
  • 2025 regime: 33% of sample at elevated returns (+8.61% D1 Open). Multi-year averages are upward-biased; 2022–2024 is more representative forward.
Caveats & Limitations
  • Survivorship bias: 6 delisted tickers excluded — likely worst performers, creating mild upward bias at longer horizons.
  • No beta adjustment: Returns are raw vs offer price. Market-wide moves contaminate the signal.
  • Korea n=20: All Korea findings are directional only — insufficient for reliable sub-group inference.
  • With more time: Beta-adjust vs regional index, multi-factor regression (region × type × discount), lock-up expiry overlay at D126.
